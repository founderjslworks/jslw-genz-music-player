Privacy Policy – GEN-Z Music

Developer: JSL WORKS PVT LTD · Effective date: 22 October 2026

GEN-Z Music is an offline music player for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. In short: the app collects nothing, shares nothing and works without the internet.

Information the app accesses

  • Audio files on your device – read only to list and play your music, show album art and lyrics, and (only when you ask) edit tags or make a ringtone.
  • Settings, playlists, favourites and play counts – stored only on your device inside the app's private storage.
  • Motion sensor – used only while music plays and "Shake to skip" or the "Gyro Droplet" effect is switched on. Sensor data is never stored.

Information we collect or share

None. GEN-Z Music has no internet permission, contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, collects no personal data or device identifiers, and sends no data to JSL WORKS PVT LTD or any third party.

Permissions

  • Music and audio / storage – to find and play your songs.
  • Notifications & foreground service – to show playback controls while music plays.
  • Display over other apps (optional) – only for floating lyrics and the full-screen lock-screen player, if you turn them on.
  • Modify system settings (optional) – only when you choose to set a ringtone you made.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All app data stays on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
